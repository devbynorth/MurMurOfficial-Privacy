Privacy information for contact, collaboration and lead-form submissions connected to PapiiDLéon ◈│◈ MurMur.

Last updated: 12 September 2026

Overview

PapiiDLéon ◈│◈ MurMur uses personal information submitted through contact and lead forms to review music, production, vocal-performance and collaboration requests.

Information we may collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Project type
  • Information you choose to provide about your project

How we use the information

We use submitted information only to:

  • review your request;
  • understand your project or collaboration;
  • contact you regarding your submission; and
  • manage relevant follow-up communication.

Submitting a form does not create an agreement, artist contract, label relationship or obligation to work together.

Sharing of information

Personal information is not sold. Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the form, website, communication or related infrastructure, including TikTok where applicable.

Retention

Information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was submitted, unless longer retention is required for legitimate administrative or legal reasons.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your submission.
